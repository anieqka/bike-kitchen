What is a Bike Repair Workshop?

Bike Repair Workshops are interactive sessions where cyclists of all levels learn maintenance skills from experienced mechanics. These community-focused events combine practical training with expert advice, helping you become more self-sufficient with your bicycle while connecting with fellow cycling enthusiasts.

Hands-on Learning

Our workshops provide practical, hands-on experience with professional guidance. You'll work directly on bikes with all necessary tools provided.

Community Building

Meet fellow cycling enthusiasts, share tips, and become part of Amsterdam's vibrant bike culture while learning valuable maintenance skills.
